Privacy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Kick Monday is run by Teaching Capital, LLC, San Diego, California. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you book, our scheduling and payment partners collect your name, email, phone number, your student's first name and grade, and your payment details. We never see your full card number. We also collect what you tell us about your student so we can coach well.

Who we share it with

Scheduling is handled by Calendly and payments by Stripe, each under their own privacy policy. Sessions run on Zoom. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else.

Cookies

This site sets one cookie-like value in your browser to remember that you have seen our cookie notice. Our booking and payment partners set the cookies they need to run checkout. We do not run advertising trackers.

Students

Our students are minors. We collect only what we need to coach them, we share nothing about a student with anyone but their parent or guardian, and we never record sessions without a parent's written permission.

Your choices

Email hello@kickmonday.com to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.
