What a session looks like

1

25 minutes with your student

Short on purpose. Your kid looks at what is actually due, builds the plan for the week themselves, and practices the one skill that will make next week easier.

2

5 minutes with you

You join at the end. You hear what we worked on, what your student committed to, and the one thing to do at home this week so you are a partner in the plan, not the enforcer.

3

We work ourselves out of a job

We teach you the same skills we teach your student, then step back as they take over. The goal is a family that no longer needs a coach.

Built by independent school teachers

We see your kid's week from inside the school

Most coaches have never run a classroom. We have both been in classrooms since 2009, in schools like your child's. We know what the teacher is about to assign, what the planner looks like by Thursday, and which habits separate the kids who are fine from the kids who are drowning quietly.

Every Kick Monday session is run by one of us. No contractors, no rotating coaches.

What happens after I pay?

You get a calendar invite with the Zoom link right away. Before the first session we send a short form so we know your student's school, grade and what the week looks like at your house.

Do I have to be on the call?

Only for the last 5 minutes. Your student has the first 25 minutes with the coach. We send you a reminder a few minutes before the debrief.

What if it isn't a fit?

Tell us after the first session and we refund the unused sessions in full.

Who runs the sessions?
